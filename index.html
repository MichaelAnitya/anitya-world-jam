About Anitya

Worlds, not websites.

Anitya is a no-code platform for creating immersive 3D Worlds. You place objects, wire up triggers, add physics and sound, bring in custom code as JSON, and publish. Your world opens on a phone, a desktop or a headset from a single link, and the people you share it with never need an account.

Our mission is to make creating a world as easy as writing a sentence — so that anyone with an idea can put people inside it.

Where we are now: open beta, live and public, improving every week. Built by the team behind the Godot engine. Blender assets import directly. Some things are still broken, and we would rather you found them than didn't.

The Main Jam

Bring any game or world idea you have and build it in Anitya. The only rule that matters: somebody should want to keep playing it. Five winners share $1,800.

What makes people keep playing

Something you can see but cannot reach yet. Something that changes because you did it. Sound that tells you where to go. A reason to walk back and look again. And give the player something to actually do.

Minimum entry: one place, one thing that changes because of the player, one reason to keep going.

Judged on

  • Fun — is it actually enjoyable to play?
  • Hold — is there always a reason to keep going? Does the next minute earn itself?
  • Interactivity — how well you use triggers, physics, gamification, sound, water, show/hide, teleports and custom code brought in as JSON
  • Sense of place — does it feel like somewhere, not just a pile of objects
  • Completeness — does it resolve, does it feel finished

We do not judge imported asset quality. We judge what you made in Anitya.

Side Quests

$200 pool · two winners per quest

Side Quests are short themed challenges that drop while the jam is running. Each theme stays sealed until the day it unlocks, so nobody gets a head start, and each one is judged on criteria announced alongside it. They are optional, they are quick, and a Side Quest entry is automatically considered for The Main Jam as well — so entering one never costs you a shot at the big pool. Every theme is announced in the Discord the moment it drops, and goes to registered creators too.

How to submit

There is no form. Submissions happen in the World Jam submissions forum channel in the Anitya Discord, so you can see what everyone else is making and they can see yours.

  1. Publish your world to the public feed in Anitya.
  2. Open a new post in World Jam submissions.
  3. Give it a title, drop in your world link, and add a screenshot or two.
  4. Say what broke. It is optional, and it is how the Founding Creator badge gets earned.

⚠Post from the Discord account you want us to contact if you win. Before winners are announced we check that the Anitya account which created the world belongs to the person who submitted it. Submitting first proves nothing, and a stolen link cannot win.

Every world you submit counts for The Main Jam. When a Side Quest is live, tagging your post with it enters both. You can submit as many worlds as you like.

Questions

Before you ask

Do I need experience with 3D?

No. Anitya is drag and drop, and you can start from a written prompt and edit what comes back. Plenty of entrants will be creating their first world. If that's you, say so when you register.

Does it cost anything?

No. Registering is free, Anitya is free to use, and there is no entry fee.

Can I enter more than one world?

Yes. Enter as many as you like, in both categories. Each is judged on its own.

Do people need an account to see my world?

No. You need a free account to create and publish. Your visitors just open the link.

Anitya is in beta. What if something breaks?

That's part of the point. Tell us — in your submission, or in the Discord. Quality of feedback and bug reports is one of the main things the Founding Creator badge is judged on.

Can I use assets I made in Blender?

Yes, GLB files import directly. But judging is on what you create in Anitya — composition, interactivity, atmosphere — not on imported asset quality.

What stops someone submitting a world that isn't theirs?

Ownership. Submitting first proves nothing. A stolen link cannot win, no matter who posted it, and submitting someone else's world gets you removed from the jam and disqualified from the Founding Creator badge. Before any winner is announced we check that the Anitya account that created the world belongs to the person who submitted it. We only do this for winners, so it costs nobody else anything. If there is a genuine reason your accounts do not line up, tell us in the Discord before submissions close rather than after.

How do I get paid if I win?

We contact you in Discord and arrange it directly. You must be 18 or over to receive a cash prize, and ownership of the winning world is verified first.

Does my world have to be new?

Yes. Build it during the jam. Worlds published before 1 September are not eligible.

What do winners get besides cash?

The best jam worlds lead the Anitya feed, which for most creators is worth more than the prize. Winners also get a Creator Spotlight feature, and the Founding Creator badge is awarded 90 days later to the creators still creating.

Can the deadline change?

It can. The organisers may extend the jam, and if that happens every participant is told at the same time — there is no private notice and no advantage in guessing. Published worlds stay live and editable, so a world you have already submitted can keep improving right up until submissions close.

What are the Side Quest themes?

Sealed until each one unlocks — that's deliberate, so nobody can prepare in advance. They are announced in the Discord the moment each one drops.
